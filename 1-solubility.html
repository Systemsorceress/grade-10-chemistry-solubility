21 · RETRIEVAL

Can you teach it back?

Solute

Substance being dissolved.

Solvent

Liquid doing the dissolving.

Solution

Solute + solvent.

Solubility

Maximum amount that can dissolve in a given amount of solvent at a particular temperature.

Saturated

Maximum dissolved solute at that temperature.

Crystallisation

Excess solute forms crystals when solubility falls.

Final rule: Solubility tells us how much solute can dissolve in a given amount of solvent at a particular temperature.
