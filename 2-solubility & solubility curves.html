02 · Two different ideas

Rate vs solubility

A substance can dissolve quickly without being able to dissolve the greatest amount.

EXAM TRAP · DO NOT CONFUSE THE TWO

RATE ≠ SOLUBILITY

Rate asks how quickly particles dissolve.
Solubility asks how much can dissolve under specified conditions.

What if the exact temperature isn't labelled?

Approximately what is it at 35°C?
35°C is halfway between 30°C and 40°C. The graph suggests a value about halfway between 45 g and 65 g: ≈55 g.

18 · Error detective

Find the mistake

EXAM TRAP · WHAT IS ACTUALLY CRYSTALLISING?
Solubility at 20°C = 40 g
Solubility at 80°C = 120 g
Therefore, when 100 g water is cooled:
120 − 40 = 80 g of water crystallises.
Find the mistake.
The water is the solvent. The changing amount is the solute that can remain dissolved. So 80 g of solute crystallises.
